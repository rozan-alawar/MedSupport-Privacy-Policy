# Privacy Policy — MedSupport Gaza

**Last updated:** May 18, 2026
**Effective date:** May 18, 2026

MedSupport Gaza is a mobile application that connects patients in Gaza with medical professionals (doctors) to facilitate remote medical consultations, appointments, and communication. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices you have.

By using MedSupport Gaza, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

### a. Account & Profile Information
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication, never accessible to us in plain text)
- Role (patient or doctor)
- Profile photo (optional)
- For doctors: specialty, medical license/credentials, years of experience, workplace, biography, consultation availability, and any documents you upload to verify your professional status.
- For patients: gender, age/date of birth, and basic medical context you choose to share with a doctor.

### b. Health & Consultation Information
- Messages, audio recordings, images, and files exchanged between patients and doctors during consultations.
- Appointment history, consultation notes, prescriptions, and medical recommendations provided by doctors.
- Any health-related information you voluntarily provide in chats or forms.

**Important:** MedSupport Gaza is a communication platform. We do not provide medical advice ourselves. Health information you share is intended for the doctor you communicate with.

### c. Device & Technical Information
- Device model, operating system, and OS version.
- Application version.
- Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver notifications.
- Time zone.
- Basic crash logs and diagnostic data to help us improve the App.

### d. Permissions You Grant
Depending on the features you use, the App may request the following permissions:
- **Camera** — to take a profile photo or capture an image for a consultation.
- **Photos / Gallery** — to attach images to a chat or upload a profile picture.
- **Microphone** — to record voice messages during a consultation.
- **Files / Storage** — to attach medical files (PDFs, documents) to a chat.
- **Notifications** — to send you appointment reminders, chat messages, and updates.
- **Internet / Network state** — required for the App to function.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device settings. Some features will not work without the relevant permission.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Allow patients to find and communicate with doctors, and vice versa.
- Deliver chat messages, voice notes, images, and files between users.
- Schedule and manage appointments and consultations.
- Send notifications (appointment reminders, new messages, system updates).
- Verify the credentials of doctors registering on the platform.
- Improve App performance, fix bugs, and develop new features.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties. We do **not** use your health information for advertising.

---

## 3. How Information Is Stored & Secured

MedSupport Gaza uses **Google Firebase** as its backend infrastructure, including:
- **Firebase Authentication** — for sign-in and password management.
- **Cloud Firestore** — for storing profiles, chats, appointments, and consultation data.
- **Firebase Storage** — for storing uploaded images, voice messages, and files.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — for delivering push notifications.

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Passwords are hashed and managed by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.

While we apply reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. You share medical and personal information at your own discretion.

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We share your information only as follows:

- **With the other party in a consultation:** Doctors see information patients share with them, and patients see information doctors choose to display (name, specialty, etc.). Chat content is visible to the two participants.
- **With service providers:** Google Firebase (Google LLC) processes data on our behalf to operate the App. Their use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of users or the public.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties for marketing.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and consultation history for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or to resolve disputes.

Chat messages, voice notes, and files associated with a consultation may be retained so that both parties continue to have access to their consultation history.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within a reasonable time frame.
